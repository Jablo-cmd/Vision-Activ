# Vision Activ — Planning & Execution Specification
**Status:** Authoritative draft | **Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 3 October 2026

## Hierarchy
Annual Goal → Quarterly Result → Monthly Result → Weekly Milestone → Daily Execution → Productive Hours.

## Planning deadlines
Quarterly plan by COB last Friday of closing quarter. Monthly plan by COB last Friday closing the month. Weekly plan for the following week by COB Friday. Monday is preparation/execution, not the place to discover the week's priorities.

## Result definition
Every significant result captures output, quality, audience, due date, effort/cost and owner.

## Prioritisation
Protect client commitments, strategic goals, revenue/margin, downstream dependencies, significant risk and material organisational impact. When priorities change, reprioritisation must be explicit.

## Execution
Support productive-hour allocation and 45-minute focus / 5-minute recovery blocks where adopted. Record progress and variance without turning time into the sole measure of contribution.

## Completion
Completion requires the defined result and required evidence, not merely activity count.
