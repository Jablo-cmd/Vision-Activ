# Vision Activ — Capability, Development & Leadership
**Status:** Authoritative draft | **Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 3 October 2026

## Capability model
Role → Required Skill → Required Proficiency → Current Demonstrated Capability → Gap → Development → Learning → Reassessment.

Portfolios: Marketing, Technology, Operations, Implementation.

## Development responses
Academy, practical assignment, coaching, mentoring, shadowing, client exposure, facilitation, technical practice, research/reading, repeated application and reassessment.

## PICC
Maximum 2–3 active priorities. Each states improvement need, different behaviour/practice, action, learning/support, demonstration/evidence and target date.

## Leadership progression
Level 1 Practitioner / Do the Job.
Level 2 Functional Leader / Manage the Function.
Level 3 Transformation Leader / Lead the Enterprise.

Progression is evidence-based: behaviour, capability, judgement, results, independence and ability to develop others.

## Succession
Track critical roles, readiness evidence, development needs and coverage. Readiness is not a title and must be based on documented evidence.
