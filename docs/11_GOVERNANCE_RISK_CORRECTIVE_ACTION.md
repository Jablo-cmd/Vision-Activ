# Vision Activ — Governance, Risk & Corrective Action
**Status:** Authoritative draft | **Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 3 October 2026

## Governance cycles
Weekly: milestones, exceptions, RAG, corrective actions.
Monthly: actuals, variances, development, next-month priorities.
Quarterly: organisational performance, risk, capability and next-quarter direction.

## Early warning
Capture issue, source, impact, likelihood/severity where approved, owner, due date, current RAG, mitigation and escalation status.

## Corrective action lifecycle
Identify variance → understand cause → define action → assign owner → set deadline → monitor → verify → close.

## Decisions and actions
A governance decision records decision, authority/context, date, owner and resulting actions. Actions have owner, deadline, status and completion evidence.

## Rules
Risks and variances should be surfaced early. Repeated failure should trigger investigation of planning, resourcing, capability, process or management causes rather than normalising overtime or last-minute recovery.
