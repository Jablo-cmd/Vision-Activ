# Vision Activ — Evidence, Assurance & Audit
**Status:** Authoritative draft | **Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 3 October 2026

## Evidence principle
Significant results require proportionate evidence. Examples include approved deliverables, final reports, tested releases, signed configurations, training evidence, approved proposals, contracts, client acceptance, financial evidence and governance decisions.

## Evidence lifecycle
Requested → Submitted → In Review → Verified/Rejected → Closed, with reopening controlled.

## Verification
The verifier must be authorised and, where independence is required, must not be the person who created the claim. Verification records actor, date, outcome and rationale.

## Audit
Audit material events: administrative changes, role changes, assessment finalisation/correction, verification, governance decisions, configuration changes and other security-significant events.

## Integrity
Do not silently overwrite historical evidence or assessment decisions. Corrections create an auditable trail.
