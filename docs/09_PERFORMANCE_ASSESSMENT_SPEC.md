# Vision Activ — Performance & Assessment Specification
**Status:** Authoritative draft | **Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 3 October 2026

## Weekly Performance Pulse
Short, delivery-connected reflection:
- committed
- achieved
- outstanding
- delivery impact
- what went well
- learning
- what changes next week
- management information/intervention

System data should prefill factual information where possible.

## Monthly Performance & Growth Review
1. Gather evidence.
2. Employee self-assesses.
3. Functional Head independently assesses.
4. Hold performance/growth conversation.
5. Agree development priorities and PICC.
6. Record outcome and follow-up.

## Protocol scale
1 Significant gap
2 Developing
3 Effective
4 Highly effective
5 Mastery

## Principles
Evidence informs judgement. Ratings do not replace conversation. Results and how results were achieved both matter. Historical assessments are preserved. Assessment exists to improve performance and capability, not to maximise scores.

## Capability assessment
Required Capability − Demonstrated Capability = Capability Gap.
Classify no material gap, development gap, significant capability risk or critical capability risk.
