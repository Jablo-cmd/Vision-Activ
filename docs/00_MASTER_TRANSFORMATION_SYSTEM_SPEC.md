# Vision Activ — Master Transformation System Specification

**Status:** Authoritative product direction  
**Version:** 1.0-draft  
**Date:** 3 October 2026  
**Source documents:** Transformation Blueprint, Transformation Passport, Transformation Academy

## 1. Purpose
Vision Activ is being evolved from a performance/self-assessment application into an internal Transformation System: the operating infrastructure that embeds planning, delivery, assessment, development, learning, evidence, governance and continuous improvement into everyday work.

The system is not primarily a scoring application. Scores are evidence-informed signals used to support judgement, conversation and improvement.

## 2. Four-part operating model
| Element | Role |
|---|---|
| Blueprint | Defines organisational standards, rules, principles and expectations. |
| Passport | Gives each person a practical reference and living transformation profile. |
| Academy | Develops knowledge, capability, judgement and mastery. |
| Transformation System | Embeds the model into planning, delivery, assessment, development and governance. |

## 3. Transformation journey
Understand → Learn → Apply → Measure → Improve → Master → Sustain

Everyday actions: PLAN → DELIVER → REVIEW → DEVELOP → LEARN.

## 4. Six organisational outcomes
1. Motivated people
2. Real accountability
3. Disciplined ways of working
4. Visible performance
5. Continuous improvement
6. Development of doers into executives

Success is material operating change, not completion of forms or accumulation of scores.

## 5. Authoritative 12 Operating Protocols
1. Accountability & ownership
2. Innovation & improvement
3. Results orientation & delivery
4. Planning & prioritisation
5. Oversight, governance & assurance
6. Focus & execution discipline
7. Lessons learned & continuous improvement
8. Decision-making & problem solving
9. Collaboration & collective leadership
10. Communication, escalation & stakeholder management
11. Client engagement & professional articulation
12. Core capability, professional presence & personal development

The existing framework-dimension implementation is a foundation to reconcile with these standards, not a competing framework.

## 6. Planning
Annual Goals → Quarterly Results → Monthly Results → Weekly Milestones → Productive Hours.

Significant work defines output, quality, audience, due date, effort/cost and owner. Plan the result before the activity. Focused execution uses 45 minutes of work followed by 5 minutes of recovery/reset.

## 7. Performance and improvement
Perform → Reflect → Assess → Identify Gap → Develop → Learn → Apply → Demonstrate → Improve.

Weekly Performance Pulse is lightweight and operational. It is not the formal monthly 12-Protocol assessment.

Monthly scale:
1 Significant gap; 2 Developing; 3 Effective; 4 Highly effective; 5 Mastery.

Evidence informs judgement; a rating never replaces the conversation.

## 8. RAG and corrective action
Green = on track. Amber = credible risk requiring corrective action now. Red = materially off track requiring major intervention.

Identify variance → Understand cause → Define action → Assign owner → Set deadline → Monitor → Verify → Close.

## 9. Performance & Growth Compact
Vision Activ provides reward, protection, opportunity, recognition and growth. Employees bring capability, accountability, discipline, performance, improvement and leadership.

## 10. Capability and leadership
Required capability → Demonstrated capability → Gap → Development → Learning → Application → Demonstration → Mastery.

Capability portfolios: Marketing, Technology, Operations, Implementation.

Leadership progression:
1. Do the Job
2. Manage the Function
3. Lead the Enterprise

Progression is demonstrated through behaviour, capability, judgement and results rather than title alone.

## 11. Academy
Four pathways:
1. Transformation Foundation
2. Operating Protocol Academy
3. Functional Capability Academy
4. Leadership & Mastery Academy

Learning lifecycle: Learn → Apply → Demonstrate → Master.

Achievement states: Learned, Applied, Mastery.

## 12. PICC
The system supports a maximum of 2–3 active PICCs per person. Each records what must improve, what will be done differently, action, learning/support, evidence and target date.

PICC connects performance, capability and Academy development.

## 13. Passport
The living record contains profile, role/function, Functional Head, leadership path, protocol profile, required versus demonstrated capability, skills/gaps, Academy modules, badges, certifications, PICC, development history and evidence/achievements.

## 14. Governance
Support weekly, monthly and quarterly reviews, risks and early warnings, decisions and actions, capability/performance visibility, lessons learned and continuous improvement.

## 15. Architecture and engineering principles
The current secure performance infrastructure is retained where semantics remain valid. New domains must be represented explicitly; the existing schema must not become overloaded.

The product is intentionally single-organisation. The explicit organisation boundary remains a security boundary and possible future configuration boundary, but multi-tenant client configuration is not current scope.

Database security is authoritative. Evidence precedes perception. Early warning precedes explanation. Improvement precedes blame. Simplicity precedes bureaucracy. Growth matters alongside gaps. Every important workflow has a defined completion state. Every requirement maps to testable acceptance criteria.
