# Vision Activ — Reporting & Executive Cockpit
**Status:** Authoritative draft | **Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 3 October 2026
## Audiences
Employee: commitments, progress, evidence, capability, learning and development. Functional Head: team performance, delivery, RAG, gaps, development and governance actions. CEO: enterprise performance, risk, capability, maturity, dependencies, decisions and transformation trends. Academy/Operations: learning, assessment, badges, critical gaps and effectiveness.
## Executive cockpit
Show direction, performance, RAG, delivery, capability, governance actions, risks, development and improvement trends. Prioritise exceptions, decisions, owners and deadlines over decorative metrics.
## Reporting rules
Reports derive from source-of-truth workflows. Manual report entries cannot become an alternative system of record. Calculated metrics must define numerator, denominator, period, population and missing-data treatment.
## Effectiveness
Track on-time delivery, quality/rework, capability change, learning application, recurring errors, independence, client feedback, protocol improvement and reduced CEO dependency.
