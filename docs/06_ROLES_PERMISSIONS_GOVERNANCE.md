# Vision Activ — Roles, Permissions & Governance
**Status:** Authoritative draft | **Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 3 October 2026

## Roles
**Employee/Practitioner:** owns personal planning, delivery, evidence, reflection, development and learning; can see authorised personal/team information.
**Functional Head/Manager:** oversees assigned function/team, verifies work where authorised, assesses performance, manages risks, development and governance actions.
**CEO/Executive:** owns enterprise direction, priorities, governance, executive decisions and transformation oversight.
**Administrator:** operates approved system administration and data-governance functions; must not gain business visibility merely because of technical administration.

## Rules
- Least privilege.
- RLS is authoritative.
- Users may access only records within their organisation and role scope.
- Self-service must not permit self-approval where independent verification is required.
- Historical finalised assessments and verified evidence require controlled correction, not silent editing.
- Functional Heads see managed people and authorised functional reporting.
- CEO sees enterprise-level information and authorised underlying detail.
- Administrative access is separated from business approval wherever practical.

## Governance
Weekly: milestones, exceptions, RAG, corrective actions.
Monthly: actual results, variance, development and next-month commitments.
Quarterly: organisational performance, risks, capability and next-quarter priorities.

System evidence supports judgement; governance meetings create decisions and accountability.

## Authority and escalation
Within mandate: decide and act. Outside mandate: Analyse → Develop options → Recommend → Escalate early.
Escalation preserves accountability; it does not automatically transfer ownership.
